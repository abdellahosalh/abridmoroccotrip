Your Winter Journey Across Morocco

From Tangier to Marrakech — a festive loop through the heart of the country

6‑Day Christmas Route
Detailed Itinerary Map
Day 1

Tangier → Chefchaouen

The Blue City

Welcome to Morocco! Your adventure begins in Tangier, the gateway to Africa. After meeting your private guide, we drive south through the rolling Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen — the famous "Blue Pearl" of Morocco. Spend the afternoon wandering through the labyrinthine blue-washed streets, photographing the vibrant doorways, and soaking in the serene mountain atmosphere. As evening falls, enjoy a traditional dinner overlooking the medina.

Travel time today: approx. 2.5 hours.

Day 2

Chefchaouen → Meknes → Cedar Forest / Azrou

Imperial City & Cedar Woods

Wake up to the crisp mountain air and enjoy breakfast with a view of the blue medina. Then we drive south to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Visit the monumental Bab Mansour gate and the Moulay Ismail Mausoleum. Continue through the Middle Atlas, passing the ski resort of Ifrane — often called "Little Switzerland" — before arriving at the Cedar Forest near Azrou. Here, you'll spot wild Barbary macaques playing among the ancient cedar trees. Overnight in a mountain lodge.

Travel time today: approx. 4.5 hours.

Day 3

Cedar Forest → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

Sahara Dunes

Leave the cedar forests behind and descend through the dramatic Ziz Valley — a lush oasis of palm groves and ancient fortified villages. Stop for panoramic photos at the Ziz Gorge viewpoint before arriving at the edge of the Sahara. In the late afternoon, mount your camel and ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes as the sun sets over the golden sand. Arrive at your luxury desert camp for a night of Berber music, traditional dinner, and stargazing under the clearest winter skies.

Travel time today: approx. 5 hours.

Day 4

Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

Canyons & Kasbahs

Wake before dawn for a sunrise camel trek back to the dunes' edge — a truly magical moment. After breakfast, drive to Rissani, the historic seat of the Alaouite dynasty, and explore its bustling souk. Then continue to the dramatic Todra Gorge, where sheer limestone cliffs rise 250 meters high. Take a walk through this natural wonder before heading to the Dades Valley, famous for its winding "Road of 1000 Kasbahs" and striking rock formations. Overnight in a traditional guesthouse with valley views.

Travel time today: approx. 4.5 hours.

Day 5

Dades Valley → Aït Benhaddou → Ouarzazate → High Atlas

Kasbahs & Film Sets

Follow the Dades Valley west, passing through the Valley of the Roses (famous for its annual rose festival). Arrive at Aït Benhaddou — the iconic UNESCO-listed ksar featured in Gladiator, Game of Thrones, and many Hollywood films. Walk through the ancient mud-brick village and climb to the top for panoramic views. Continue to Ouarzazate, the "Hollywood of Morocco," with an optional visit to the film studios or Taourirt Kasbah. Then ascend the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass — the highest road in Morocco. Overnight in a mountain guesthouse.

Travel time today: approx. 4 hours.

Day 6

High Atlas → Marrakech

Red City Finale

Enjoy one last mountain breakfast before descending through the High Atlas toward Marrakech. The drive offers breathtaking views of the snow-capped peaks — a winter wonderland contrast to the desert you've just explored. Arrive in Marrakech and dive into the vibrant energy of the Red City. Your tour ends after lunch, with the option to explore the souks, Jemaa el-Fna square, or book an extension to discover more of Marrakech's palaces and gardens.

Travel time today: approx. 3 hours.

Optional add-on: Extend your stay in Marrakech with a hot air balloon ride at sunrise, a cooking class, or a guided tour of the Bahia Palace and Majorelle Garden.

6-Day Christmas in Morocco - Frequently Asked Questions

What is the weather like in Morocco in December?
Expect crisp, sunny days with cool evenings. The Atlas highlands may have snow, which is spectacular, while Marrakech stays mild - perfect for a festive escape.
Is this tour family-friendly?
Yes - the 6-day Christmas tour is popular with families, couples, and small groups. The private vehicle and relaxed pacing work well with children of all ages.
Can I extend the tour?
Of course. Add extra days in Marrakech, Essaouira, or the Sahara desert before or after the itinerary - just let us know your plans.
Where does the tour start?
The tour starts with pickup in Marrakech on Day 1 and ends with drop-off back in Marrakech on Day 6. Transfers from other cities can be arranged.
How do I book for Christmas dates?
Availability is limited over the Christmas period, so message us on WhatsApp with your dates early. As always, no deposit is required - payment in cash goes to your guide on the day.
