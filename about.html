Meet Your Morocco Experts

Two passionate locals with deep roots and endless knowledge – from the High Atlas to the Sahara and beyond.

Abdellah

📍 Imilchil (High Atlas)
🌟 Berber Guide · 5 Languages

I was born and raised in Imilchil, a stunning Berber village in the High Atlas Mountains famous for its twin lakes (Isli & Tislit) and the legendary wedding festival. I've traveled extensively to every corner of Morocco – from the Mediterranean coast to the Sahara – and I've guided travelers from the USA, UK, Spain, Germany and Australia. I speak 5 languages – Tamazight (Berber), Arabic, French, English, and Spanish – and I love sharing the authentic culture, hospitality, and hidden stories of my homeland.

Karim Ousgho

📍 Sahara Desert
🌟 National Explorer

I'm a Marrakech native with a burning passion for exploring every corner of Morocco. I've traveled extensively across the entire country – from the blue streets of Chefchaouen and the Mediterranean coast of Tétouan, to the Atlantic vibes of Agadir and Essaouira, the golden dunes of the Sahara, and the cinematic landscapes of Ouarzazate. I know the souks like the back of my hand, the best rooftop terraces, and the hidden riads that make Morocco magical. I'm passionate about sharing authentic experiences and ensuring every guest feels like family.
