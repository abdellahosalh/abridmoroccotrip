Meet Your Morocco Experts

Abdellah

📍 Imilchil (High Atlas)
🌟 Berber Guide · 5 Languages

I was born and raised in Imilchil, a stunning Berber village in the High Atlas Mountains famous for its twin lakes (Isli & Tislit) and the legendary wedding festival. I've traveled extensively across every corner of Morocco – from the Mediterranean coast to the Sahara – and I've guided hundreds of travelers from the USA, UK, Spain, Germany, Australia, and across Europe. I speak 5 languages – Tamazight (Berber), Arabic, French, English, and Spanish – and I love sharing the authentic culture, hospitality, and hidden stories of my homeland.
