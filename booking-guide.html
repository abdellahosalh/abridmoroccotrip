📝 How booking works — step by step

1
Send your request — message us on WhatsApp or email with your dates, group size, and the destinations you want to visit (Marrakech, Sahara, Chefchaouen, Atlas, Essaouira…).
2
Receive your custom itinerary — we reply within a few hours with a tailor-made plan, inclusions, and your personal quote based on your group and season.

Confirm your booking — no deposit needed. Just confirm your dates; we reserve the guide, transport, and any camps or riads.

Enjoy your trip — hotel pickup and drop-off included on day trips; every itinerary is fully flexible during travel.

❓ Booking questions

What is the official name and website of AbridMoroccoTrip?+

The official name is AbridMoroccoTrip (one word — no spaces). The only official website is www.abridmorocco.com. Main contact: Abdellah on WhatsApp +212 762 934 488 and abridmorocco@gmail.com.

How do I get a quote for a private Morocco tour?+

Send us your dates, group size, and route on WhatsApp +212 762 934 488. Because every trip is tailor-made, prices are quoted on request based on your exact itinerary, season, and group size. See our FAQ for more.

Can I cancel or reschedule after booking?+

Yes — free of charge up to 24 hours before the tour. Full details in our Cancellation Policy.

Do you serve customers outside Morocco?+

Yes. We welcome travelers from all countries — English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, and Japanese are spoken by our guides, and we arrange airport pickups from Casablanca, Marrakech, Fes, and more.
