Fes Medina Guided Day Tour

The world's largest car-free medina, decoded by a licensed local guide: tanneries, madrasas, the Mellah and the pottery quarter.

📅 Full day in Fes 🧭 Licensed local guide 🏨 Riad pickup included 💬 Quote on request
Plan This Tour Ask About Availability

How the day goes

Morning: pickup from your riad or hotel and a panoramic start at the Merinid tombs viewpoint over the whole medina. Then descend on foot into Fes el-Bali with your licensed guide: the Bou Inania and Al-Attarine madrasas, the dye pits and weaving quarters, and the famous Chouara tannery terraces.

Midday: pass the Al-Qarawiyyin mosque-university — admired from the outside, as non-Muslim visitors do not enter — and lunch in a medina restaurant at your own pace.

Afternoon: the Mellah (historic Jewish quarter) with its distinct architecture, the pottery and zellige quarter where artisans still fire and cut tile by hand, and the souks for brass, leather and textiles — with honest guidance on fair prices and no pressure to buy.

Evening: return to your riad. Without a guide the medina is a maze; with one, it becomes the most rewarding day of a Morocco trip.

Where it is

Fes in northern Morocco — the oldest imperial capital. Drag to explore.

Frequently asked questions

Do I really need a guide in Fes?
The medina has over 9,000 alleys and is genuinely easy to get lost in. A licensed guide also opens doors — workshops, terraces and rooftops you would never find alone.
Can non-Muslims enter the mosques?
No — mosques like Al-Qarawiyyin are admired from outside. The madrasas, open to all visitors, show the same architecture from the inside.
Is there pressure to shop?
Not with us. Your guide shows artisan quarters as craftsmanship first; buying is entirely your call, with advice on fair prices.
