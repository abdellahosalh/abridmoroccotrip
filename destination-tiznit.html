Tiznit: Silver Town

Silver jewellery souks behind old walls — Tiznit is the craft town on the way to Mirleft's beaches.

?? Silver jewellery ?? Old medina walls ??? Near Mirleft beaches ?? Souss plain

Silver behind the walls

Tiznit's name is synonymous with silver across Morocco — the jewellery souk gathers Berber fibulae, anklets and daggers from workshops across the south, and bargaining here is calmer than in any big city. The walled medina around it is compact, authentic and barely touristed.

The town sits on the road to Mirleft's beaches and pairs with Taroudant for the full Souss pair. Come for the silver, stay for the slow southern rhythm.

Highlights

  • Silver souk — Berber jewellery from southern workshops.
  • Medina walls — the old rampart circuit.
  • Grand Mosque quarter — the town's historic heart.
  • Craft workshops — metalwork and woodwork lanes.
  • Beach road — Mirleft's coves a short drive west.
How we recommend experiencing Tiznit. Morning silver souk with your driver to translate and bargain fairly, medina walk, afternoon at the Mirleft beaches. Good to know

How to get there

Tiznit sits south of Agadir on the Souss plain, served by buses and grand taxis from Agadir and Taroudant. With Abrid, it folds into a southern coastal loop with private transport.

How long to stay

We recommend half a day for the silver souk and medina, or one night paired with Mirleft's beaches.

Best time to visit

Year-round sunshine; spring and autumn are generally the mildest for souk wandering and beach days.

Tiznit questions

Is Tiznit silver real?

The souk trades genuine Berber silver alongside newer pieces — quality varies, which is exactly why you shop with a local who knows the workshops. Your driver helps you buy fairly.

How long does Tiznit need?

Half a day for the silver souk and medina walls, or one night paired with Mirleft's beaches. Most travellers combine it with Taroudant.
