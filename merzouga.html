Merzouga Desert Adventure — 3 Days Camel Trek & Camp

Merzouga Desert Adventure (3 Days / 2 Nights)

Experience the magic of the Sahara: camel trek, desert camp, sunset over dunes, and Berber hospitality.

Request a quote Tailored to your group and travel dates

What's Included

  • Transport Marrakech → Dades Valley → Merzouga → Marrakech
  • Licensed guide – fluent in English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, or Japanese
  • 1 night hotel in Dades Valley (dinner & breakfast)
  • 1 night desert camp (dinner & breakfast)
  • Camel trek at sunset & sunrise
  • Sandboarding on dunes

Not Included

  • Lunch on Day 1 and Day 3
  • Drinks

Itinerary

Day 1: Marrakech → Dades Valley (stops at Ait Benhaddou, Ouarzazate). Overnight hotel with dinner.

Day 2: Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Merzouga. Camel trek at sunset, overnight in desert camp with dinner & music.

Day 3: Sunrise camel trek, breakfast, return to Marrakech (arrival evening).

Merzouga Desert Tour - Frequently Asked Questions

How do you get from Marrakech to Merzouga?
We drive in a private, air-conditioned vehicle along the dramatic route through the High Atlas, stopping at Ait Benhaddou, Ouarzazate, and the Dades Valley. The full journey to the Sahara takes about two days in total with overnights included.
Is the Sahara desert tour suitable for children?
Yes. Families regularly join this tour, and children love the camel trek and sandboarding. The 4x4 is comfortable, and our guides adapt the pace to your group. Just mention children's ages when you request a quote.
What is the desert camp like?
You stay in a traditional luxury desert camp with comfortable tents, mattresses, bedding, hot showers, and dinner and breakfast cooked by our Berber hosts. Overnights in Dades are in a comfortable hotel.
What should I pack for the Sahara?
Light layers for warm days, and a warm layer for the cooler evening and night. Bring sunglasses, sunscreen, and closed shoes for the dunes. See our complete Sahara packing guide.
How do I book the Merzouga tour?
Send us your travel dates and group size on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability and a quote. No deposit is required - you pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) to your guide on the day.
