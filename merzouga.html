3‑Day Merzouga Desert Tour from Marrakech

Sahara camel trek, luxury desert camp, Dades Gorge & Todra Gorge. All meals and accommodation included. Experience the real Sahara with a local Berber guide.

Request a quote Personalised for your group and travel dates

Full Itinerary – Day by Day

Day 1: Marrakech → Dades Valley

  • 07:30 – Pickup from your Marrakech hotel in a comfortable air‑conditioned 4x4.
  • 09:30 – Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n’Tichka pass (2,260m) – photo stop.
  • 11:30 – Visit the UNESCO‑listed Ait Benhaddou ksar (Game of Thrones filming location).
  • 13:00 – Lunch break in Ouarzazate (optional, not included).
  • 15:00 – Scenic drive through the Valley of the Roses and the Dades Valley.
  • 18:00 – Arrive at your hotel in the Dades Valley.
  • 19:30 – Welcome dinner (included) and overnight at the hotel.
  • Driving time: ~6‑7 hours with stops.

Day 2: Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Merzouga (Camel Trek & Desert Camp)

  • 08:00 – Breakfast at the hotel.
  • 09:00 – Drive to the spectacular Todra Gorge – walk through the 300m high limestone cliffs.
  • 11:30 – Continue towards Merzouga, passing through the Berber villages.
  • 14:00 – Arrival in Merzouga – leave your main luggage at the hotel and prepare for the desert.
  • 16:00 – Camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes (1.5 hours) – watch the sunset over the golden sands.
  • 19:00 – Arrive at the desert camp – a luxury camp with private tents and en‑suite bathrooms.
  • 20:00 – Traditional Berber dinner (included) followed by music around the campfire.
  • Overnight – Sleep under a starry sky in the heart of the Sahara.

Day 3: Merzouga → Marrakech

  • 05:30 – Wake up for a sunrise camel trek – climb the highest dune for spectacular views.
  • 07:30 – Return to the camp for breakfast (included).
  • 09:00 – Ride back to Merzouga village.
  • 09:30 – Depart for Marrakech via the same scenic route (lunch stop en route).
  • 19:00 – Arrive in Marrakech and drop‑off at your hotel.
  • Driving time: ~9 hours with stops.

What's Included

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for children? +
Absolutely! Children are welcome on our desert tours. The camel ride is gentle and suitable for kids aged 6 and above. For younger children, we can arrange a 4x4 transfer to the camp instead of the camel ride. We also have family‑friendly tents with extra space.
What about luggage storage? +
You can leave your large suitcases safely at the hotel in Merzouga village. We provide a small overnight bag for your essentials (camel ride takes only a small backpack).
Is it safe to travel to the Sahara? +
Yes, the Sahara region is very safe for tourists. Our local guides are experienced and know the terrain well. We also have support vehicles in case of emergencies. Morocco is a stable and welcoming country.
Are there bathroom facilities at the desert camp? +
Yes! Our luxury camp offers private en‑suite bathrooms with running water, flushing toilets, and hot showers. You can enjoy the desert without sacrificing comfort.
Can I travel to the Sahara in summer? +
Summer (June–August) is very hot – temperatures can reach 45°C during the day. If you travel in summer, we recommend starting the camel trek later in the afternoon to avoid the heat. The nights are still pleasant. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are the best times for comfortable temperatures.
What should I pack for the desert? +
We recommend: comfortable closed‑toe shoes, light layers (the desert gets cold at night), a warm jacket, sunscreen, sunglasses, a headscarf for sand protection, and a power bank for your phone. See our complete packing guide for more details.
How do I book and pay? +
You can book via our contact form or WhatsApp. No deposit required – you pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) directly to your guide on the first day of the tour.
