Merzouga Desert Adventure (3 Days / 2 Nights)

Experience the magic of the Sahara: camel trek, desert camp, sunset over dunes, and Berber hospitality.

$145 per person

What's Included

  • Transport Marrakech → Dades Valley → Merzouga → Marrakech
  • Licensed guide – fluent in English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, or Japanese
  • 1 night hotel in Dades Valley (dinner & breakfast)
  • 1 night desert camp (dinner & breakfast)
  • Camel trek at sunset & sunrise
  • Sandboarding on dunes

Not Included

  • Lunch on Day 1 and Day 3
  • Drinks
  • Tips

Itinerary

Day 1: Marrakech → Dades Valley (stops at Ait Benhaddou, Ouarzazate). Overnight hotel with dinner.

Day 2: Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Merzouga. Camel trek at sunset, overnight in desert camp with dinner & music.

Day 3: Sunrise camel trek, breakfast, return to Marrakech (arrival evening).
