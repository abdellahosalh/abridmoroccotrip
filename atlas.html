Atlas Mountains & Ourika Valley Day Trip

Escape to the stunning Atlas Mountains. Visit Berber villages, enjoy scenic views, and experience local culture.

€100 per person · final price depends on group size

Why choose this Atlas Mountains tour?

The Atlas Mountains are the backbone of Morocco — a majestic range that separates the Sahara from the Atlantic coast. This day trip takes you into the heart of the High Atlas, where you'll experience authentic Berber village life, walk through stunning valley landscapes, and enjoy a traditional meal with a local family.

Unlike crowded tourist buses, our private 4x4 tour gives you the freedom to stop at the best viewpoints, explore at your own pace, and discover hidden spots that most visitors miss. Our local Berber guides know every trail and will share stories that bring the mountains to life.

Full Itinerary (8 hours)

  • 09:00 – Pickup from your Marrakech hotel
  • 09:45 – Drive through the Ourika Valley, stopping for panoramic views
  • 11:00 – Visit a traditional Berber village – meet locals and learn about daily life
  • 12:00 – Guided walk to waterfalls (gentle hike, 30–45 minutes)
  • 13:30 – Lunch with a Berber family – home‑cooked tajine, fresh bread, and mint tea
  • 15:00 – Free time to explore, take photos, or relax
  • 16:00 – Return drive to Marrakech
  • 17:00 – Drop‑off at your hotel

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the hike to the waterfall difficult?
No, it's a gentle hike suitable for most fitness levels. The walk is about 30–45 minutes each way on a clear path. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended.
What does the traditional Berber lunch include?
A traditional tajine (chicken or vegetable), fresh salads, bread, and mint tea — all cooked fresh by a local Berber family. Vegetarian options are available on request.
What is the best time of year to visit?
The Atlas Mountains are beautiful year‑round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the best weather. In winter, there may be snow on the higher peaks which is spectacular.
Can children join this tour?
Yes! Children love the waterfall and the village visit. The 4x4 vehicle makes the journey very comfortable. Children under 8 get a 50% discount.
Do we get free time to explore on our own?
Yes — after lunch, you'll have free time to explore the village, take photos, or simply relax and enjoy the mountain views before the return journey.
How do I pay?
You pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) directly to your guide on the day of the tour.
