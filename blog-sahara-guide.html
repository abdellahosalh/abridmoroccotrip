The Complete Guide to the Sahara Desert

By Abdellah | Local Tour Expert | March 20, 2026

No trip to Morocco is complete without experiencing the quiet majesty of the Sahara Desert. The rolling dunes stretching infinitely into the horizon, the deep silence of the night, and the glowing campfire beneath a sky blanketed in stars—it is a transformative experience.

Merzouga (Erg Chebbi) vs Zagora

When booking a tour to the Sahara from Marrakech, you will usually be offered two destinations: Zagora or Merzouga.

Zagora is a 2-day, 1-night trip. It is closer, but the desert here is flat and rocky. There are very few sand dunes. If you are extremely short on time, it's an option.

Merzouga (Erg Chebbi) is a 3-day, 2-night trip. This is the Sahara you are dreaming of. The dunes here tower hundreds of feet high into the sky. The sand is a deep, glowing orange. We at AbridMoroccoTrip exclusively run tours to Merzouga because it is vastly superior.

What to Pack for the Sahara

Packing for the desert requires preparing for extreme temperature shifts. Here is a quick list of essentials:

  • Layers! The desert is scorching in the day, but near freezing at night in winter. Bring a warm jacket.
  • A headscarf (Cheich). This isn't just for photos. The Berber wrap protects your face from sand and wind during your camel ride.
  • Closed-toe shoes. Sandals are fine for the camp, but you'll want solid shoes for climbing the dunes.
  • Wet wipes and a power bank. Camp electricity can be limited!

The Drive from Marrakech

Be prepared: Merzouga is incredibly far from Marrakech. It takes about 10 hours of driving. However, the drive itself is one of the best parts of the journey!

You will cross the dramatic High Atlas Mountains via the winding Tizi n'Tichka pass. You'll stop at the ancient, mud-brick fortress of Ait Benhaddou (where Gladiator and Game of Thrones were filmed). You will see the sheer cliff walls of the Todra Gorge. The road trip is just as important as the destination!
