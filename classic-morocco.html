Classic Morocco 13-Day Itinerary

Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen → Fes → Sahara Desert → Todra Gorge → Ait Benhaddou → Marrakech

Camel trek, desert camp, henna workshop, and authentic Berber experiences.

Day 1: Casablanca – Arrival Hotel (1 night)

Included: Complimentary arrival transfer.

Optional: Villa des Arts Gallery (Free), Jewish Museum (Contact for pricing).

Special Info: Welcome meeting – collect insurance and next of kin details.

Day 2: Casablanca → Rabat → Meknes Hotel (1 night)

Included: Hassan II Mosque, Kasbah des Oudaias, Hassan Tower, Mausoleum Mohammed V, Mausoleum Hassan II.

Travel Time: ~3.5 hours.

Day 3: Meknes → Volubilis → Chefchaouen Riad (1 night)

Included: Meknes Medina walking tour, Volubilis Entrance & Guided Tour, Chefchaouen leader-led orientation walk.

Travel Time: ~4.5 hours.

Day 4: Chefchaouen → Fes Hotel (1 night)

Included: Free morning in Chefchaouen. Evening dinner in Fes – try the salty & sweet pastilla!

Travel Time: ~4 hours.

Day 5: Fes – Full Day Guided Tour Hotel (1 night)

Included: Fes el Bali medina walk, Funduq al-Najjarin, Medersa El Attarine.

Special Info: Shopping in the medina – commissions are centralized and distributed fairly.

Day 6: Fes → Midelt Hotel (1 night)

Included: Scenic drive through the High Atlas, Midelt valley hike.

Travel Time: ~3.5 hours.

Day 7: Midelt → Sahara Desert (Merzouga) Desert Camp (1 night)

Included: Camel ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes, sunset over the Sahara, traditional dinner, Berber music under the stars.

Optional: 4WD drive instead of camel (Contact for pricing per person).

Day 8: Sahara → Todra Gorge → M'Goun Valley Gite / Homestay (1 night)

Included: Sunrise over the dunes, Todra Gorge leader-led walk, transfer to M'Goun Valley, homestay with a Berber family.

Travel Time: ~7 hours.

Day 9: M'Goun Valley – Hike & Henna Workshop Gite (1 night)

Included: 4-hour guided hike (10 km circuit) through Amazigh villages, henna tattooing activity with a local naquasha.

Day 10: M'Goun → Skoura → Ait Benhaddou Guesthouse (1 night)

Included: Road of 1000 Kasbahs, Skoura Oasis & Kasbah Ameridhl visit, Ait Benhaddou leader-led ksar walk, Tawesna Tea House visit (community-run).

Travel Time: ~4 hours.

Day 11: Ait Benhaddou → Marrakech Riad (1 night)

Included: Scenic drive through the High Atlas passes, photo stops, evening free to explore Jemaa el-Fna.

Travel Time: ~3.5 hours.

Day 12: Marrakech – Full Day Guided Tour Riad (1 night)

Included: Medina walking tour, Bahia Palace, Koutoubia Mosque (exterior), fresh orange juice at Jemaa el-Fna.

Optional: Majorelle Gardens (Contact for pricing), Palais Badi (Contact for pricing), Saadian Tombs (Contact for pricing), Medersa Ben Youssef (Contact for pricing), Museum of Marrakech (Contact for pricing).

Day 13: Marrakech – Departure

Included: Breakfast. Free time to explore or add optional activities (hot air balloon, cooking class, etc.).

Optional: Hot Air Balloon Ride (Contact for pricing), Tajine Cookery Class (Contact for pricing).
