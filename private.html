Private Morocco Tours - Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private Morocco tour cost?
Because every private tour is tailor-made around your dates, group size, and route, prices are quoted on request. Contact us on WhatsApp with your plans and we will send a clear quote.
Can you customize the itinerary?
Yes - that is the whole point of a private tour. You can mix Marrakech, the Sahara, the Atlas, Chefchaouen, and the Imperial Cities in any order, and adjust daily distances and hotels.
Which languages do your guides speak?
Our licensed guides speak English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, and Japanese. Tell us your preferred language when booking.
Is a deposit required?
No. We never require a deposit - you pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) to your guide at the start of the trip, giving you total flexibility.
Can my private tour start outside Marrakech?
Absolutely. We arrange pickup and departures from Casablanca, Fes, Essaouira, and other cities - see our airport transfer page for arrival logistics.
