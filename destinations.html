❓ Morocco destination questions

What is the best Morocco desert tour from Marrakech?+

The classic 3-day route — Marrakech → Aït Benhaddou → Ouarzazate → Dades → Todra Gorge → Merzouga/Erg Chebbi with a desert camp night → return via the High Atlas. See our Merzouga Desert Adventure.

How many days do you need in Morocco?+

For Marrakech plus a Sahara trip, plan 7–10 days. For a full circuit including Fes, Chefchaouen and the Imperial Cities, 10–14 days — like our Classic 13-Day Morocco tour.

Is Merzouga worth visiting?+

Yes — Erg Chebbi's golden dunes are Morocco's classic desert experience: camel trek at sunset, traditional camp, and stars over the Sahara. It's the most scenic and accessible dune field.

What is the best month to visit Morocco?+

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) for pleasant temperatures. Summer suits the coast and mountains; winter can bring snow to the Atlas and cooler desert nights.

Which is better: Merzouga or Zagora?+

Merzouga/Erg Chebbi has bigger, more dramatic dunes and is the classic choice. Zagora is more convenient from Marrakech but has smaller dunes. See Erg Chebbi vs Erg Chigaga.

How far is Marrakech from the Sahara?+

Marrakech to Merzouga is about 560 km — roughly a day's drive via Aït Benhaddou and Ouarzazate, which is why the 3-day tour includes intermediate stops. We handle all transport.
