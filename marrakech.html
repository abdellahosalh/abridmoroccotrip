Marrakech Discovery Tour

Explore the vibrant souks, historic palaces, and beautiful gardens of Marrakech with a local guide.

€50 per person · final price depends on group size

Why choose this Marrakech tour?

Marrakech is a city that captivates all your senses — the smell of spices, the vibrant colours of the souks, the sound of snake charmers and storytellers. But without a local guide, it can feel overwhelming. Our Marrakech Discovery Tour is designed to give you the perfect balance: insider knowledge, enough free time to explore on your own, and a stress-free experience.

We'll take you to the must‑see highlights (Bahia Palace, Jemaa el‑Fna, the souks) and also share hidden corners that most tourists miss — like the quiet Mellah (Jewish quarter) and the best rooftop spots for sunset mint tea. All with a friendly, English‑speaking guide who knows the city inside out.

Full Itinerary (6 hours)

  • 09:00 – Pickup from your hotel / riad
  • 09:30 – Koutoubia Mosque (exterior) – learn about its history and architecture
  • 10:15 – Explore the souks (markets) – we'll help you navigate and bargain fairly
  • 11:30 – Visit Bahia Palace (entry fee extra, but worth it)
  • 12:45 – Free time at Jemaa el‑Fnaa square – grab lunch or a juice
  • 14:30 – Rooftop mint tea stop (optional, our favourite café)
  • 15:00 – Return to your hotel

What's included

  • ✓ Air‑conditioned transport
  • ✓ Licensed local guide (English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, or Japanese)
  • ✓ Hotel pickup and drop‑off
  • ✓ Insider tips on bargaining, safety, and hidden gems

Not included: Lunch, monument entrance fees, tips.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for children?
Yes! This tour is family‑friendly. The walking pace is relaxed and we adapt to your group. Ask us about arrangements for children when booking.
Is it safe to walk through the souks?
Absolutely. Marrakech is very safe for tourists. With our local guide you will feel completely comfortable – he knows all the safe routes and will handle any pushy sellers.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your tour to reschedule for free. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a small fee.
What should I wear?
We recommend comfortable walking shoes and modest clothing (shoulders and knees covered) out of respect for local culture. This is especially important when visiting mosques and palaces.
Do you speak Spanish?
Yes! Our guides speak English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, and Japanese. Just let us know your preferred language when booking.
How do I pay?
You pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) directly to your guide on the day of the tour.
