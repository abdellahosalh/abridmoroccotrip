Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for children? +
Yes! This tour is family-friendly. The walking pace is relaxed and we adapt to your group. Children under 12 get a 50% discount.
Is it safe to walk through the souks? +
Absolutely. Marrakech is very safe for tourists. With our local guide you will feel completely comfortable – he knows all the safe routes and will handle any pushy sellers.
Can I cancel or reschedule? +
Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your tour to reschedule for free. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a small fee.
What should I wear? +
We recommend comfortable walking shoes and modest clothing (shoulders and knees covered) out of respect for local culture. This is especially important when visiting mosques and palaces.
Do you speak Spanish? +
Yes! Our guides speak English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, and Japanese. Just let us know your preferred language when booking.
