Private Marrakech Discovery Day Tour

Explore the souks, palaces, and hidden gems of Marrakech with a local guide. Visit Bahia Palace, the bustling Jemaa el-Fna, and the vibrant souks — all at your own pace.

Request a quote Personalised for your group

Full Itinerary – 6 Hours

Morning: Koutoubia & Souks

  • 09:00 – Pickup from your hotel or riad in Marrakech.
  • 09:30 – Visit the Koutoubia Mosque (exterior) – learn about its 12th‑century Almohad architecture and the iconic minaret that dominates the city skyline.
  • 10:15 – Dive into the souks – we'll guide you through the labyrinth of stalls selling spices, leather, lanterns, and carpets. We'll help you navigate and bargain fairly.
  • 11:30 – Visit the stunning Bahia Palace – a 19th‑century masterpiece with 150 rooms, intricate cedar wood ceilings, and colourful zellij tiles.

Afternoon: Jemaa el-Fna & Hidden Gems

  • 12:45 – Free time at Jemaa el-Fna square – grab a fresh orange juice or explore the food stalls (lunch not included).
  • 14:00 – Discover the Mellah (Jewish quarter) – a quiet, historic neighbourhood with beautiful architecture and a fascinating history.
  • 14:30 – Rooftop mint tea stop at a hidden café – our favourite spot with views over the medina.
  • 15:00 – Return transfer to your hotel.

Walking distance between stops: ~15‑20 minutes total. Comfortable shoes recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for children? +
Absolutely! This tour is family‑friendly. The walking pace is relaxed and we adapt to your group's needs. Children are welcome and we can adjust the itinerary to keep them engaged.
Is it safe to walk through the souks? +
Yes, Marrakech is very safe for tourists. With our local guide you will feel completely comfortable – he knows all the safe routes and will handle any pushy sellers professionally.
What is the meeting point? +
We pick you up directly from your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Just let us know your accommodation name and address when booking.
Can I cancel or reschedule? +
Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your tour to reschedule for free. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a small fee.
What should I wear? +
We recommend comfortable walking shoes and modest clothing (shoulders and knees covered) out of respect for local culture. This is especially important when visiting palaces and religious sites.
How do I book and pay? +
You can book via our contact form or WhatsApp. No deposit required – you pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) directly to your guide on the day of the tour.
