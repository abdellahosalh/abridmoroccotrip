Top 10 Things to Do in Marrakech

Published March 15, 2026

Marrakech, the Red City, is a feast for the senses. From the moment you step into the medina, you're surrounded by vibrant colors, exotic scents, and the buzz of daily life. Here are the top 10 things you absolutely cannot miss.

1. Explore the Souks

The labyrinthine souks of Marrakech are a shopper's paradise. Lose yourself in alleys filled with carpets, lanterns, spices, and leather goods. Don't forget to haggle!

2. Visit Bahia Palace

A stunning 19th-century palace with intricate tilework, courtyards, and gardens. A must-see for architecture lovers.

3. Koutoubia Mosque

The largest mosque in Marrakech, with a beautiful minaret visible from miles away. Non-Muslims cannot enter, but the gardens are lovely.

4. Jemaa el-Fna

The main square comes alive at night with food stalls, storytellers, and musicians. Try a bowl of snail soup or fresh orange juice.

5. Majorelle Garden

A serene garden designed by French painter Jacques Majorelle, later owned by Yves Saint Laurent. The striking blue buildings are iconic.

6. Saadian Tombs

Hidden for centuries, these tombs date back to the 16th century and feature beautiful marble and cedar carvings.

7. Ben Youssef Madrasa

A historic Islamic college with stunning architecture, now open to visitors.

8. Take a Food Tour

Sample traditional Moroccan dishes like tagine, couscous, and pastilla. Join a guided food tour for the best experience.

9. Relax in a Hammam

Experience a traditional Moroccan bath and massage. It's the perfect way to unwind after a day of sightseeing.

10. Day Trip to the Atlas Mountains

Escape the city hustle and enjoy breathtaking mountain views, Berber villages, and waterfalls. Book our Atlas Mountains tour for a hassle‑free experience.
