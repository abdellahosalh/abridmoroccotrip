Chefchaouen Day Trip from Fes

The Blue Pearl of the Rif in one full day: blue-washed lanes, the kasbah square, and the Spanish Mosque viewpoint at sunset.

📅 Full day from Fes 🚐 Private A/C transport 🧭 Local medina guide 💬 Quote on request
Plan This Trip Ask About Availability

How the day goes

Morning: early pickup from your riad or hotel in Fes. The drive north crosses the Rif foothills — roughly four hours each way — with a comfort stop on the road.

Midday: arrive in Chefchaouen and meet your local medina guide. Walk the blue-washed lanes, Plaza Uta el-Hammam with its kasbah walls, and the historic core. Lunch at your own pace in a medina restaurant (your guide suggests reliable local addresses).

Afternoon: free time for photography and the small artisan shops, then the short walk up to the Spanish Mosque for the classic sunset panorama over the blue town. Optional extension toward the Akchour waterfalls for walkers with more time — ask in advance.

Evening: drive back to Fes, drop-off at your accommodation. A long day, but the only way to see the Blue Pearl without changing hotels.

Where it is

Chefchaouen sits in the Rif Mountains, about four hours north of Fes. Drag to explore.

What's included

  • Private A/C transport Fes ↔ Chefchaouen with driver, including fuel
  • Hotel/riad pickup and drop-off in Fes
  • Local medina guide in Chefchaouen
  • Spanish Mosque viewpoint walk

What's not included

  • Lunch, drinks and snacks
  • Monument entry fees (e.g. kasbah museum, if visited)
  • Tips for driver and guide
  • Personal expenses

Frequently asked questions

Is one day enough for Chefchaouen?
Yes for the medina, kasbah square and Spanish Mosque viewpoint. Stay overnight only if you want the Akchour waterfalls or a slower pace — we can arrange that too.
Can I start from another city?
Yes — Chefchaouen also works from Tangier or as a stop between Fes and Tangier. Tell us your route when you enquire.
Is the Spanish Mosque walk difficult?
It is a short uphill walk of about 30 minutes on a clear path — easy for most walkers, with the best panorama as the reward.
