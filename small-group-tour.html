11-Day Morocco Discovery

Small Group Tour

From Casablanca to Marrakech — imperial cities, the blue pearl of Chefchaouen, thundering waterfalls, and a camel ride through the Palmeraie. Max 22 travelers.

📅 11 Days / 10 Nights 👥 Max 22 Travelers 🍽️ 21 Meals Included 🏨 10 Nights Accommodation 🇲🇦 Rabat · Tangier · Fez · Marrakech

🇲🇦 Tour Overview

Experience the best of Morocco on this carefully curated small group journey. From the Atlantic coast to the Atlas foothills, you'll explore ancient medinas, bustling souks, royal palaces, and serene mountain landscapes — all with the comfort of a small group and the expertise of local guides.

🌟 Why This Tour

Thanks to our small group size (max 22 travelers), we can get to the heart of our destination, visit places that other tours miss, and get to know the locals. You'll enjoy a more intimate, authentic experience with plenty of flexibility.

⭐ Tour Highlights

🏛️ Explore Rabat — Royal Palace, ancient ruins of Chellah, and the Oudaya Kasbah
🏰 Visit Asilah — a fortified town on the Atlantic Coast, dating back to 1500 BC
🌍 Tour Tangier — the city that served as Europe's gateway to Africa for centuries
🏔️ Explore Tetouan — a multicultural city in the foothills of the Rif Mountains
🔵 Discover Chefchaouen — Morocco's enchanting "Blue City" dating back to the 15th century
🕌 In Fez — walk through the maze of lanes in the bustling Fes el Bali Medina with its souks and tanneries. Join a Fez family for a dinner of home-cooked fare in their home
💦 Marvel at Ouzoud Waterfalls — an impressive 330-foot high natural wonder
🌿 Admire Majorelle Gardens — a mirage of over 300 plant species from five continents, and Le Jardin Secret
🍳 Attend a cooking class in an authentic Moroccan Riad
🐪 Enjoy a camel ride through the Palmeraie, a palm oasis outside of Marrakesh

ℹ️ Is This Trip Right for You?

Things to Consider: This tour includes animal-related activities which carry inherent risks, as animal behavior can be unpredictable. You may choose not to participate. Expect several days with early mornings. This tour is not recommended for people who have difficulty walking. Most meals served on tour are traditional Moroccan cuisine with limited selections. Vegetarian options are limited and typically consist of vegetarian tagines, couscous, soups, legume stews, and a variety of cooked and raw salads.

Transportation: There are two days with over 5 hours of driving.

Pace & Terrain: Expect 3–4 hours of walking on included sightseeing days, as well as considerable time standing. You may need to walk on uneven and challenging terrain, navigate steep steps or slopes, and encounter cobblestone streets.

Travel Style: This is a Small Group program. For more information, contact us.

Small Group Morocco Tour - Frequently Asked Questions

How big are the small groups?
Our small-group Morocco tour keeps group sizes small and comfortable - typically around 8-12 travelers - so you get personal attention and an easier trip.
Can I join as a solo traveler?
Yes. Solo travelers are very welcome, and the small group format is an easy way to explore Morocco while meeting like-minded travelers.
What is included in the small-group tour?
As shown in the itinerary: comfortable hotels and riads, transport, a licensed guide, and key meals on most days. Full inclusions are confirmed when you request a quote.
Is this tour different from a private tour?
Yes. Small-group tours share the journey with a fixed group at a more accessible rate, while private tours are fully tailored to you alone. Ask us which fits the trip.
How do I pay?
No deposit is required. You pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) to your guide at the start of the tour as agreed.
