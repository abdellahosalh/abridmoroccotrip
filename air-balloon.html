Hot Air Balloon Ride over Marrakech

Soar above the palm groves and desert at sunrise for an unforgettable view of the Atlas Mountains and the Red City.

$250 per person (shared flight)
$450 private flight (for 2)

What's Included

  • Early morning pickup from your Marrakech hotel
  • Traditional Berber breakfast at the launch site
  • Approximately 1-hour hot air balloon flight
  • Flight certificate to commemorate your experience
  • Expert, licensed pilot (English/French speaking)
  • Refreshments after landing
  • Return transfer to your hotel

Not Included

  • Tips for the crew
  • Personal expenses

Itinerary

Duration: Approximately 4-5 hours (including transfers)

  • **4:30 AM:** Pickup from your Marrakech hotel.
  • **5:30 AM:** Arrive at the launch site, enjoy a traditional Berber breakfast and mint tea while the balloon is being prepared.
  • **6:30 AM:** Take flight at sunrise! Soar over the palm groves, Berber villages, and desert landscape with the Atlas Mountains as your backdrop.
  • **7:30 AM:** Gentle landing and a celebration with the crew.
  • **8:00 AM:** Receive your flight certificate and enjoy more refreshments.
  • **9:00 AM:** Transfer back to your hotel.

Why Choose This Experience?

This is the perfect way to see the vastness of Morocco's landscape and get a unique perspective of Marrakech. It's a popular choice for couples celebrating a honeymoon or anniversary, and for any traveler wanting to add a touch of magic to their trip. The calm of floating above the world at sunrise is an unforgettable memory.
