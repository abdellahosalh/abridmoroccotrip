Hot Air Balloon over Marrakech — Sunrise Flight

Hot Air Balloon Ride over Marrakech

Soar above the palm groves and desert at sunrise for an unforgettable view of the Atlas Mountains and the Red City.

Request a quote Shared and private flight options available

What's Included

  • Early morning pickup from your Marrakech hotel
  • Traditional Berber breakfast at the launch site
  • Approximately 1-hour hot air balloon flight
  • Flight certificate to commemorate your experience
  • Expert, licensed pilot (English/French speaking)
  • Refreshments after landing
  • Return transfer to your hotel

Not Included

  • Tips for the crew
  • Personal expenses

Itinerary

Duration: Approximately 4–5 hours (including transfers)

  • 4:30 AM: Pickup from your Marrakech hotel
  • 5:30 AM: Arrive at launch site — traditional Berber breakfast & mint tea while balloon is prepared
  • 6:30 AM: Takeoff at sunrise! Soar over palm groves, Berber villages & desert with Atlas Mountains backdrop
  • 7:30 AM: Gentle landing and celebration with the crew
  • 8:00 AM: Receive your flight certificate & refreshments
  • 9:00 AM: Transfer back to your hotel

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe? +
Yes. We use only fully licensed and certified pilots with extensive experience. All equipment is regularly inspected and meets international aviation safety standards. We have an excellent safety record.
What if the weather is bad? +
Flights operate only in safe weather conditions. If weather forces a cancellation, we will reschedule your flight to the next available date or issue a full refund — your choice.
What should I wear? +
Comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. Mornings can be cool before sunrise — bring a light jacket. The flight itself is gentle with no extreme temperatures.
Is this good for a honeymoon or anniversary? +
This is one of our most popular romantic experiences! The private flight option gives you a completely private balloon and is perfect for couples celebrating a special occasion. Many propose during the flight!
