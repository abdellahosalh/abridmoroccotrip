Hot Air Balloon Ride over Marrakech

Soar above the palm groves and desert at sunrise for an unforgettable view of the Atlas Mountains and the Red City.

$260 per person (shared flight)
$450 private flight (for 2)

What's Included

  • Early morning pickup from your Marrakech hotel
  • Traditional Berber breakfast at the launch site
  • Approximately 1-hour hot air balloon flight
  • Flight certificate to commemorate your experience
  • Expert, licensed pilot (English/French speaking)
  • Refreshments after landing
  • Return transfer to your hotel

Not Included

  • Tips for the crew
  • Personal expenses

Itinerary

Duration: Approximately 4–5 hours (including transfers)

  • 4:30 AM: Pickup from your Marrakech hotel
  • 5:30 AM: Arrive at launch site — traditional Berber breakfast & mint tea while balloon is prepared
  • 6:30 AM: Takeoff at sunrise! Soar over palm groves, Berber villages & desert with Atlas Mountains backdrop
  • 7:30 AM: Gentle landing and celebration with the crew
  • 8:00 AM: Receive your flight certificate & refreshments
  • 9:00 AM: Transfer back to your hotel
