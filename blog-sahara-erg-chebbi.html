Erg Chebbi vs Erg Chigaga: Which Sahara Dunes to Visit?

Published March 16, 2026

If you're planning a Sahara desert trip from Marrakech, you'll quickly encounter two names: Erg Chebbi (near Merzouga) and Erg Chigaga (near Zagora). Both offer incredible desert experiences, but they're quite different. Which one is right for you?

I've guided tours to both deserts for years. Here's my honest comparison to help you decide.

🏜️ Quick Overview

Feature Erg Chebbi (Merzouga) Erg Chigaga (Zagora)
Distance from Marrakech ~560 km (9 hours drive) ~400 km (7 hours drive)
Dune height Up to 150 meters Up to 100 meters
Accessibility Easy – paved road to village Remote – last 2 hours off-road
Tourism development High – many camps, hotels Low – wilder, fewer camps
Best for First-timers, comfort seekers Adventurers, solitude seekers

📍 Erg Chebbi (Merzouga) – The Popular Choice

Erg Chebbi is Morocco's most famous dune field, located near the village of Merzouga. These massive golden dunes rise up to 150 meters and stretch for about 50 kilometers. Most travelers choose Merzouga for their first Sahara experience – and for good reason.

✅ Pros of Erg Chebbi:

  • ✓ Spectacular high dunes – incredibly photogenic
  • ✓ Easy access – paved road to Merzouga village
  • ✓ Many camp options – from basic to luxury
  • ✓ Camel trekking from the village
  • ✓ Amenities nearby (shops, ATMs, restaurants)

❌ Cons of Erg Chebbi:

  • ✗ More tourists (can feel crowded)
  • ✗ Commercialized in some areas

📍 Erg Chigaga (Zagora) – The Wild Alternative

Erg Chigaga is located near Zagora, about 7 hours from Marrakech. The dunes are slightly smaller but spread over a vast area. The journey itself is an adventure – the last 2 hours are on rough desert tracks.

✅ Pros of Erg Chigaga:

  • ✓ More remote and wild – fewer tourists
  • ✓ True desert solitude experience
  • ✓ Off-road adventure to reach it

❌ Cons of Erg Chigaga:

  • ✗ Harder to reach – 4x4 required
  • ✗ Fewer camp options
  • ✗ Dunes not as high as Erg Chebbi
  • ✗ Longer, bumpier drive

🤔 Which One Should You Choose?

Choose Erg Chebbi (Merzouga) if... Choose Erg Chigaga (Zagora) if...
✅ It's your first desert trip ✅ You've visited deserts before
✅ You want stunning photos ✅ You crave true solitude
✅ You prefer comfort and options ✅ You love off-road adventures
✅ You have limited time (3 days) ✅ You have 4+ days for tour

🚗 Sample Itineraries

Erg Chebbi (Merzouga) 3-Day Tour:
Day 1: Marrakech → Dades Valley (hotel night)
Day 2: Dades → Merzouga → Camel trek → Desert camp
Day 3: Sunrise trek → Return to Marrakech

Erg Chigaga (Zagora) 4-Day Tour:
Day 1: Marrakech → Ait Benhaddou → Ouarzazate
Day 2: Ouarzazate → Zagora → Desert camp
Day 3: Desert exploration → Return to Zagora
Day 4: Zagora → Marrakech

✅ Our Recommendation

For 95% of travelers, Erg Chebbi (Merzouga) is the better choice. The dunes are more spectacular, access is easier, and you get the full Sahara experience without sacrificing comfort. That's why we specialize in Merzouga tours.
