Ouzoud Quad Tour

Ride guided quad bikes through the red and purple trails of the Atlas mountains, visit Ouzoud Waterfalls, dinosaur footprints and traditional Berber villages — with a perfect 5.0/5.0 record across 99 verified guest reviews.

Request a quote 1-hour · 2-hour · 3-hour tours · open daily 9:00–19:30

Why ride at Ouzoud?

Ouzoud is home to one of Morocco's most beautiful waterfall systems, and its rugged Atlas surroundings are perfect for quad biking. Wind through palm groves and mountain tracks to the head of the springs, the old Berber town, and historic sites — including real dinosaur footprints. It's an adventure for the whole family: we have 1, 2 and 3-hour options and we'll happily adapt the pace and driving arrangements to your group.

As a locally run outfit (known in Ouzoud as Abrid Quad Aventure), the same team behind every Abrid Morocco tour leads your ride. Guests consistently mention safety, knowledgeable local guides, and the chance to meet Amazigh families over traditional tea and msemen.

What's included

  • Guided quad bike tour — 1, 2 or 3-hour option
  • Safety-first local guide (English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, Japanese)
  • Ouzoud Waterfalls & head of the springs viewpoints
  • Old Berber town and historical / archaeological sites (dinosaur footprints)
  • Traditional tea and msemen break with a local family
  • Hotel pickup & drop-off within Ouzoud

Not included: Personal expenses, tips.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the quad tour safe?
Yes. Safety is our guests' most repeated highlight — guides prioritize safety throughout the ride, brief you before setting off, and lead every trail themselves. Please wear comfortable, closed-toe shoes.
Do I need experience to ride a quad?
No experience needed. We offer 1, 2 and 3-hour tours, pair you with the right quad for your group, and let you take turns driving — perfect for families and teens.
Where is the tour?
Meeting point is within Ouzoud (22000), Morocco — we also pick up at Ouzoud hotels. The trails run through the High and Middle Atlas around Ouzoud Waterfalls.
How do I book and pay?
Book via WhatsApp (+212 762 934 488) or the booking form. You pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) on the day. See our Booking Guide for details.
