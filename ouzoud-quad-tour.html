Why Ouzoud?

  • Spectacular Waterfalls — one of Morocco's most beautiful waterfall systems.
  • Diverse Terrain — winding trails through red and purple tracks, palm groves, and rugged mountain landscapes.
  • Cultural Immersion — visits to traditional Berber towns and encounters with local Amazigh people.
  • Unique Landmarks — historical and archaeological sites, including dinosaur footprints.
