Agadir – Morocco’s Beach City

Sunshine, long Atlantic beaches and a laid-back seaside town — a different, more modern Morocco for those who want the coast, the surf and the Souss Valley.

RegionSouss Valley, Atlantic coast south
Best timeYear-round; mild winters
VibeBeach resort, surf, marina
NearbyTaghazout surf, Paradise Valley

Agadir is Morocco’s big beach city — rebuilt after the 1960 earthquake around a bay of golden sand under the Anti-Atlas. It is the choice for travellers who want looser days: a long promenade, relaxed cafés, a marina and easy access to the surf town of Taghazout.

Beyond the beach, the region offers the deep bergamot-green gorges of Paradise Valley, the old port of Essaouira’s southern cousin Sidi Ifni on a longer extension, and the low-key walled town of Taroudant to the east. It also pairs naturally with a route that reaches the Sahara further south.

“Agadir is where the trip breathes. No labyrinth medina, no touts — just the Atlantic, long lunches and the Souss in the distance.” — Karim, guide
