Morocco Overview & Trip Logistics

Morocco is a North African kingdom where ancient medinas, snow-capped Atlas Mountains, and golden Sahara dunes exist within hours of each other. This 7-day route threads through the country's most iconic destinations giving you a genuine taste of Moroccan culture, cuisine, and landscape without rushing.

Best visited: October–April (avoid summer heat; Sahara hits 45°C in July).
Budget: $300–400 total for budget travelers; $600–800 for comfort seekers.

Day 1-2: Marrakech — The Red City

Day 1: Arrival & Medina Immersion

Morning: Land at Marrakech Menara Airport. Take petite taxi (~70 MAD to medina — do NOT take grand taxis). Breakfast at café near riad — msemen with argan oil (~20–30 MAD).

Afternoon: Walk to Jemaa el-Fna Square. Explore the Chouara Souks. Visit Bahia Palace (entry 70 MAD).

Evening: Return to Jemaa el-Fna at sunset for food stalls (50–80 MAD total). Try snail soup (harira), lamb skewers, orange juice (4 MAD!). Rooftop mint tea at Café des Épices.

Insider tip: Don't walk into the Koutoubia Mosque courtyard as a non-Muslim — just photograph from outside. Save 20 MAD in "entry fees" that are actually fake.

Day 2: Gardens, Palaces & Food Tour

Morning: Majorelle Garden (entry 150 MAD) — arrive 8:30 AM before crowds. Walk through the Mellah (Jewish quarter).

Afternoon: El Badi Palace ruins (entry 70 MAD). Hammam experience at Hammam El Bacha — traditional local hammam, 200 MAD with scrub.

Evening - Depart for Sahara: Take 7 PM bus toward Merzouga.

Day 3-4: Sahara Desert — Merzouga Dunes

Day 3: Arrival & Sunset Camel Ride

Morning: Arrive ~8–9 AM. Check into your desert hotel and rest during the afternoon heat.

Afternoon/Evening: Camel ride departs 4:30–5 PM. Sunset from highest dune is unmissable. Spend the night in a luxurious desert camp under a blanket of thousands of stars. Enjoy Berber music around a glowing campfire.

Desert tour scam alert: Do NOT pay roadside touts. Book through a trusted official partner like Abrid Morocco Trips.

Day 4: Sunrise & Todra Gorge

Pre-dawn: Wake at 5:30 AM, climb dune for sunrise.

Afternoon – Todra Gorge (optional): Walk the gorge for 1 hour, then travel toward Fès.

Day 5: Fès — The Medieval Medina

Morning: Fès el-Bali medina, a UNESCO site. Visit Al-Qarawiyyin Library. Bou Inania Madrasa (entry 20 MAD).

Key Attraction – Chouara Tannery: Enter through leather shops for free overhead views. Best light 10 AM–12 PM.

Afternoon: Bab Bou Jeloud (Blue Gate). Get lost — 9,400 lanes is part of the experience.

Evening: Travel to Chefchaouen.

Medina navigation: Download offline Google Maps BEFORE entering. Cell signal drops in narrow alleys.

Day 6: Chefchaouen — The Blue City

Best Photo Spots: Blue staircase (Rue Targui) at 7–8 AM; Spanish Mosque viewpoint (35-min hike, sunset view).

Food: Breakfast: msemen with goat cheese and honey. Lunch: Berber omelette.

Afternoon: Travel toward Casablanca.

Why blue? Painted blue in the 15th century as a symbol of heaven and the sky above.

Day 7: Casablanca — Grand Finale

Morning – Hassan II Mosque: World's 7th largest mosque, built on the Atlantic. Non-Muslims can enter (entry 120 MAD). Cover shoulders and knees.

Afternoon: La Corniche beachfront promenade; Rick's Café (from the movie); Morocco Mall for fixed-price souvenirs.

Departure: Airport Express train to Mohammed V International Airport.

Casablanca insider: Most tourists rush through. Spend your final evening at Ain Diab beach — watch sunset over the Atlantic.
