Mount Toubkal Summit Trek

Stand on the roof of North Africa at 4,167 m: two days from Imlil with a certified mountain guide, mule support and a refuge night.

📅 2 Days / 1 Night ⛰️ Summit 4,167 m 🧭 Certified guide + mules 💶 €700 per person

Day 1

Marrakech → Imlil → Refuge

The climb begins

Morning: pickup in Marrakech and drive to Imlil (1,740 m) at the end of the tarmac road. Meet your certified mountain guide and mule team, then trek up the Aït Mizane valley — past walnut groves and Berber hamlets — to the mountain refuge (~3,200 m).

Afternoon: settle into dormitory accommodation, hot lunch, and an early rest. Your guide briefs the summit plan over mint tea.

Evening: dinner at the refuge and an early night — summit day starts before dawn.

Day 2

Summit 4,167 m → Imlil → Marrakech

Summit day

Morning: pre-dawn start for the summit push — scree slopes, then the famous iron pyramid at the top of North Africa as the sun rises over the Atlas. Descend to the refuge for breakfast and rest.

Afternoon: trek back down to Imlil, transfer to Marrakech, drop-off at your accommodation with very tired legs and a summit to remember.

Fitness note: summit day is long and steep with significant altitude gain. Regular hikers manage it; it is not a stroll. Tell us honestly about your level and we advise accordingly.

Frequently asked questions

When is Toubkal climbable?
Late spring to early autumn for standard trekking. Winter ascents need ice axe, crampons and experience — ask us about winter conditions.
Do I need a guide?
Yes — a certified mountain guide is required on Toubkal, and mules carry the heavy loads. Both are included in our trek.
How fit do I need to be?
Summit day means roughly 1,000 m of ascent before breakfast and a long descent. Fit hill-walkers do well; complete beginners should train first.
