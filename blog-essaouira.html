The Ultimate Guide to Essaouira: Morocco's Windy City

By Abdellah | Local Tour Expert | March 24, 2026

Few places capture the soul as effortlessly as Essaouira. This white-and-blue coastal town, formerly known as Mogador, is a breath of fresh Atlantic air—literally. Known for its steady trade winds and laid-back atmosphere, it's the perfect antidote to the high energy of Marrakech.

1. Walk the Historic Ramparts (Skala de la Ville)

Built in the 18th century to protect the city from sea attacks, these ramparts offer stunning views of the Atlantic. You'll recognize them as a filming location for 'Game of Thrones'. It's the best spot for a sunset walk while watching the seagulls dance in the wind.

2. Visit the Port and the Blue Boats

The Essaouira fishing port is famous for its vibrant blue wooden boats. Watch local fishermen bring in the catch of the day, mend their nets, and sell fresh sardines. The atmosphere here is authentic, raw, and incredibly photogenic.

3. Explore the Labyrinthine Medina

Unlike Marrakech, Essaouira's medina is easy to navigate because of its grid-like structure. It's filled with workshops where craftsmen carve beautiful objects from Thuya wood. Don't forget to look for the many art galleries that line the white-washed streets.

4. Feast on Fresh Seafood

Head to the fish stalls near the port. You pick your fish or lobster directly from the ice, and they grill it for you on the spot. Served with simple bread and salad, it's one of the most memorable meals you'll have in Morocco.

5. Try Kitesurfing or Windsurfing

Essaouira is a world-class destination for wind sports. Whether you're a pro or a beginner, the beaches offer plenty of schools where you can rent gear or take a lesson. The "Alize" wind makes it a surfer's paradise nearly all year round.
