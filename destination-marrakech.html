The Red City – Marrakech

Palace courtyards, maze-like souks, rooftop views over Jemaa el-Fnaa and easy access to the High Atlas. Marrakech is where most Morocco journeys begin.

RegionSouth-west, near the High Atlas
Best timeMar–May & Sep–Nov
VibeHistoric city, markets, day-trip hub
Base forSahara, Atlas & coast trips

Marrakech is the gateway to southern Morocco. Around the UNESCO-listed medina you will find the 12th-century Koutoubia Mosque, the ornate Bahia Palace, the serene Majorelle Garden and the ever-moving theatre of Jemaa el-Fnaa — storytellers, musicians, food stalls and snake charmers at dusk.

Beyond the walls, the same city is the launch point for the classic routes we run: desert experiences towards Merzouga, mountain days in the Ourika Valley, hot air balloon flights at sunrise, and a day at the Atlantic in Essaouira.

“Marrakech rewards slow mornings: mint tea on a rooftop, the call to prayer drifting over the medina, then a long lunch with a view.” — Abdellah, founder & Berber guide

Highlights

Jemaa el-Fnaa

The famous square — try it at sunset for food stalls, storytellers and music.

The Souks

Endless lanes of leather, spices, carpets and lamps — go early and bargain kindly.

Palaces & Gardens

Bahia Palace, El Badi and the Majorelle Garden for calm and colour.

Rooftop Culture

Atlas views, mint tea and slow afternoons above the medina rooftops.
