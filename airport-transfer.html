✈️ Free Airport Transfer

Start your Morocco adventure stress‑free. We offer a complimentary private transfer from the airport to your riad or hotel in Casablanca, Marrakech, Fes, or any other city.

🚗 Private & Comfortable

🤝 Personal Service from Abdellah or Karim

We don't just arrange your transfer – we personally make sure everything is perfect.

  • 🏠 On the day of your tour, Abdellah or Karim will come to your riad, hotel, or hostel.
  • 🚶 They will personally walk you to your driver and ensure the vehicle and guide are ready.
  • 💰 They will collect the remaining payment (if any) and provide you with a receipt.
  • 📱 They will stay in contact with you throughout your entire journey – any question, any time.
  • 📞 You'll have their direct WhatsApp number for instant support.

How It Works

1

Book Your Tour

After clearing customs, proceed to the arrival hall. Your driver will be there with a sign.

3

  • ✈️ Provide your flight number and arrival time at least 48 hours before departure.

  • 🎁 Transfer is free for all guests on our tours. Additional stops (e.g., supermarket) can be arranged.

Airport Transfer in Morocco - Frequently Asked Questions

Which airports do you serve?
We offer private transfers to and from all major Moroccan airports and cities - Casablanca (CMN), Marrakech (RAK), Fes (FEZ), Agadir (AGA), and Tangier (TNG).
What happens if my flight is delayed?
We track every flight. If yours is delayed, your driver waits at no extra cost, so you never miss your pickup.
What vehicles do you use?
Modern, air-conditioned vehicles chosen for your group size - from comfortable sedans to 4x4s and minivans for families and larger groups.
Do you also transfer hotel to airport?
Yes - we handle the return trip too, picking you up from your riad or hotel in good time for your departing flight.
How do I book and pay for a transfer?
Send us your flight details on WhatsApp for a free quote. Pay in cash (EUR, USD, or MAD) to your driver.
