Azrou: Cedars & Macaques

Ancient cedar forests with wild Barbary macaques — Azrou is the Middle Atlas at its greenest, on the road south from Ifrane.

🌲 Cedar forests 🐒 Wild macaques ⛰️ Middle Atlas 📍 Ifrane → Midelt road

Into the cedar shade

South of Ifrane, the road enters one of Morocco's great cedar forests — centuries-old trees where troops of wild Barbary macaques come down to the roadside. Azrou itself is a working mountain town famous for its cedar-wood artisans, with sawmills and carving workshops along the main street.

Almost every Fes-to-desert route passes here, which makes Azrou the easiest wildlife stop in Morocco: no trek, no detour — the monkeys meet the road. Pair it with Ifrane and the onward drive to Midelt and the Ziz Valley.

Highlights

  • Cedar forest — ancient trees south of town.
  • Barbary macaques — wild troops by the roadside.
  • Cedar artisans — carving workshops on the main street.
  • Mountain viewpoints — the Middle Atlas rolling south.
  • Weekly souk — a genuine mountain market day.
How we recommend experiencing Azrou. A one-hour forest stop with your driver: macaques, cedars, artisan browse — then onward. How long: one hour passing through; half a day with the souk. What travellers miss: the carving workshops — cedar craft at roadside prices. How we connect it: Ifrane ? Azrou ? Midelt is the classic desert-bound morning.

Good to know

How to get there

Azrou sits on the Ifrane–Midelt road, served by buses and grand taxis from both. With Abrid, the forest stop is built into your Fes-to-desert driving day.

How long to stay

We recommend one hour for the forest and macaques, half a day with the souk and artisans. Almost nobody sleeps here — and that is fine.

Best time to visit

Year-round; the forest is greenest in spring, snowy and silent in winter. Macaques are around in every season.

Azrou questions

Will I actually see macaques?

Very likely — the troops habituate the roadside forest and your driver knows the current spots. Mornings are usually best. They are wild animals: watch, don't feed.

Is Azrou worth a stop?

Yes as a one-hour forest break on the Fes–desert road — cedars, monkeys and artisans with zero detour. Not a base, a pause.
