Imilchil, Lakes Tislit & Isli & Mount Bab N'Ouayad Summit Tour

3 Days / 2 Nights High Atlas Mountain Expedition. Summit Bab N'Ouayad (2,804m), explore sacred Twin Lakes Isli & Tislit, and hike through the dramatic Assif Melloul Gorges.

$150 per person (All-inclusive)

What's Included ($150 / Person)

  • Comfortable, air-conditioned tourist transport throughout the journey
  • Comprehensive tourist transport insurance
  • 2 Nights hotel / authentic mountain lodge accommodation in Imilchil
  • All Meals Included:
    • Saturday: Healthy breakfast + Picnic lunch by Lake Tislit + Traditional dinner
    • Sunday: Energizing breakfast + Farewell full lunch
  • Certified professional mountain guide throughout the trip
  • Professional shooting & group photo service
  • Official Ascension Certificate for Mount Bab N'Ouayad (2,804m) signed by your guide
  • Full logistical coordination, trip supervision & guidance

Not Included

  • Friday evening dinner (free choice during highway rest stop)

Full Detailed Program (3 Days / 2 Nights)

📅 Day 1: Friday — Departures from Rabat & Casablanca → Imilchil

  • 17:30 — Departure from Rabat (Gare Rabat Agdal).
  • 19:00 — Departure from Casablanca (Gare Casa-Voyageurs).
  • Dinner break at a highway rest station (free choice / or bring your cold meal).
  • Late evening arrival at our hotel in Imilchil (2,119m altitude).
  • Check-in, room dispatching, and a peaceful overnight stay in the High Atlas.

📅 Day 2: Saturday — Summit Bab N'Ouayad (2,804m), Twin Lakes Isli-Tislit & Picnic

  • Morning: Wake-up & healthy balanced breakfast at the hotel (Included).
  • Short scenic transfer to the hiking trailhead.
  • Ascent of Mount Bab N'Ouayad: Reach the majestic peak at 2,804m altitude (+600m elevation gain, ~2 hours ascent, Difficulty level: 2/5).
  • Summit Moment: Glory celebration & photo session with an extraordinary 360° panoramic view overlooking both turquoise lakes: Lake Tislit & Lake Isli.
  • Descent and transfer to the shores of Lake Tislit.
  • Picnic Lunch by Lake Tislit: Enjoy a delicious picnic lunch surrounded by the towering peaks of the Eastern High Atlas range (Included).
  • Visit Lake Isli: Explore the grand lake and listen to the romantic and tragic Amazigh love legend of the two lakes told by our expert guide.
  • Optional Activity: Mountain Biking (VTT) circuit between Lakes Isli and Tislit (Optional: 200 MAD).
  • Return to the hotel: Refreshing hot shower & relaxation.
  • Traditional Berber Dinner: Enjoy a warm authentic meal at the hotel (Included).
  • Evening entertainment: Friendly board games, cozy atmosphere, and starry night sky.
  • Overnight stay at the hotel (Included).

📅 Day 3: Sunday — Assif Melloul Gorges, Summit Certificates & Return

  • Wake-up and breakfast at the hotel (Included).
  • 30-minute transfer to the stunning Assif Melloul Gorges.
  • Canyon Hike: Easy 1-hour walk (Level 1/5) into the heart of the Assif Melloul Gorges with multiple scenic stops to admire valleys, mountain caves, and dramatic cliffs — perfect for photography.
  • Return to the hotel for a delicious Farewell Lunch (Included).
  • Official Awards: Presentation of the official Mount Bab N'Ouayad Summit Ascension Certificate (2,804m) signed by our mountain guide (Included).
  • Departure for the return journey to Casablanca and Rabat with sweet memories!

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to take the Imilchil tour? +
Spring through autumn (May to October) offers warm sunny days and pleasant weather. September is particularly famous for the annual Imilchil Wedding Festival. In winter, snow transforms the plateau into a winter wonderland.
Can this tour start from Marrakech or Fes? +
Yes! We can pick you up and drop you off in Marrakech, Fes, Beni Mellal, or Casablanca. We can also customize the route as a connecting trip between Marrakech and Fes.
Is the hiking difficult? +
The walks around Lake Tislit and Isli are gentle to moderate and customized to your fitness level. Non-hikers can also enjoy the scenic 4x4 viewpoints.
What should I bring? +
Comfortable walking shoes, sunglasses, a hat, sunscreen, and warm layers (jacket/fleece) because mountain temperatures drop significantly at night.
