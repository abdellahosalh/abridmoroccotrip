How much does a private trip cost?

Every itinerary is quoted individually — pricing depends on destinations, season, group size and level of stay. We send your exact quote with the proposed route, and you only pay in cash on tour day.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

You pay in cash to your guide when the trip starts. This is how we build trust: you see the whole plan and price first, then pay on the day.

Is Morocco safe for solo travelers?

Morocco is generally safe for American and European travelers, including solo women. Use common sense, dress modestly and travel with trusted local guides. Read more in our safety FAQ.

Can the itinerary be changed once I'm there?

Yes — your driver-guide is flexible and can adjust stops or pace during the trip. That said, most travelers keep the agreed plan and simply enjoy it.
