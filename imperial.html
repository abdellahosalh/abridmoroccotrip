Imperial Cities Circuit (8 Days / 7 Nights)

Discover Morocco's rich history in Fez, Marrakech, Rabat, and Meknes.

$2,803 per person

What's Included

  • All transport in air-conditioned coach
  • Multilingual guide – fluent in English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Russian, or Japanese
  • 7 nights in 4-star hotels/riads
  • 7 breakfasts, 6 dinners
  • All entrance fees to monuments

Not Included

  • Lunches (except Day 1)
  • Drinks
  • Tips

Itinerary

Day 1–2: Marrakech – guided tour of souks, Bahia Palace, Koutoubia

Day 3: Travel to Fez via Ifrane, overnight in Fez

Day 4: Fez guided tour (medina, tanneries, Al Quaraouiyine University)

Day 5: Day trip to Meknes and Volubilis (optional)

Day 6: Travel to Rabat – Hassan Tower and Royal Palace

Day 7: Casablanca (Hassan II Mosque), return to Marrakech

Day 8: Departure

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the group size? +
This tour runs as a private tour for your group only — no sharing with strangers. The air-conditioned coach accommodates up to 16 people. Ideal for families, couples, and small groups.
What is the Fez medina like? +
Fez's medina is one of the largest and most complex medieval cities in the world with over 9,000 alleys. Without a professional guide you WILL get lost. Our guide knows every corner and the best photo spots.
Are the 4-star hotels comfortable? +
We use carefully selected 4-star riads and hotels with en-suite bathrooms, air conditioning, and wifi. Many are historical buildings with beautiful courtyard gardens — a real experience in themselves.
Can I customize the itinerary? +
Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss your preferences. We can add the Sahara desert, Chefchaouen, or Essaouira to create your perfect Morocco circuit.
