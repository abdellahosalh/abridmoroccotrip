Ouzoud Camel Ride

A guided camel ride on the plateau trails around Ouzoud — Atlas views, village paths and mint tea, beside the waterfalls.

🐪 Guided camel ride 🏔️ Atlas viewpoints 🍵 Mint tea stop 💬 Quote on request
Plan This Experience Ask About Availability

How it goes

Meeting: meet your cameleer at an agreed point near the falls village — most riders combine this with our Ouzoud Waterfalls day trip, riding before or after the falls walk.

The ride: a gentle guided loop on the plateau trails above the valley, with open views toward the Atlas foothills and the village fields below. Helmets and handling are covered before you mount; the pace is a walk throughout.

After: mint tea at a viewpoint terrace, then free time for the waterfalls themselves. Total experience time around two hours including tea.

Where it is

The trails around Ouzoud in the Atlas foothills. Drag to explore.

What's included

  • Guided camel ride with local cameleer
  • Safety briefing and handling
  • Mint tea stop at a viewpoint

What's not included

  • Transport (add our Ouzoud day trip for the full day)
  • Personal expenses
  • Tips for the cameleer

Frequently asked questions

Do I need riding experience?
No — camels walk slowly in line with the guide, and the briefing covers everything before you mount.
Is it suitable for children?
Yes, children ride with an adult or led by the guide. Tell us ages when booking so we prepare.
Can I combine it with the waterfalls visit?
That is exactly how most travellers do it — ride first, then walk the falls, or the reverse. Ask for the combined day.

Tell us your date — we confirm cameleer and timing within 2 hours.
