Marrakech to Essaouira: Bus vs. Private Tour — Which is Better?

✍️ By Abdellah📅 May 24, 2026📍 Essaouira Coast

If you are staying in Marrakech, taking a day trip to the coastal town of Essaouira is an absolute must. Known as the "Windy City of Africa," it offers a relaxed vibe, beautiful ocean views, incredible fresh seafood, and an old Medina that fans of Game of Thrones will instantly recognize!

But what is the best way to get there? The drive is about 2.5 to 3 hours each way. Here is a breakdown of your two main options: The public bus vs. a private tour.

Option 1: The Public Bus (Supratours or CTM)

  • The Pros: It is cheap. A one-way ticket costs around 80 to 100 MAD (about $8-$10). The buses are generally clean and air-conditioned.
  • The Cons: You are on a strict schedule. You have to pay for a taxi from your Riad to the bus station in Marrakech, wait in line, and sit on a crowded bus. The bus makes one quick stop at a busy rest stop, but you cannot pull over to take photos of the famous "Goats in the Argan Trees" (yes, goats literally climb trees here!).

Option 2: A Private Guided Trip

  • The Pros: Total freedom and comfort. Your driver picks you up directly from your Riad in Marrakech. You control the AC and the music. More importantly, you can stop wherever you want! We always stop to see the tree-climbing goats and visit a local women's Argan oil cooperative to see how "Moroccan liquid gold" is made.
  • The Cons: It costs a little more than the public bus, but the value and time saved are huge.

Why You Should Choose the Private Option

Your vacation time is precious. Spending 6 hours of your day waiting in bus stations and riding with 40 strangers is exhausting. With our Essaouira Coastal Escape ($95/person), you get VIP treatment. We handle the driving in a private, comfortable vehicle, show you the hidden stops along the way, and give you plenty of free time to explore Essaouira's beautiful Medina and eat fresh seafood by the harbor.
