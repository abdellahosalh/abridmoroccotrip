Ouzoud Waterfalls Day Trip

North Africa's great waterfall in one day from Marrakech: top and bottom viewpoints, wild macaques, and an optional boat ride to the cascades.

📅 Full day from Marrakech 🚐 Private A/C transport 🐒 Barbary macaques 💶 €150 per person
Plan This Trip Ask About Availability

How the day goes

Morning: pickup from your accommodation in Marrakech and drive into the Atlas foothills — roughly three hours each way through olive groves and Berber villages.

Midday: arrive at the falls, which drop around 100 metres in a series of cascades. Walk the rim path to the top viewpoints, then descend the stepped trail past cafés and viewpoints to the pools below. Wild Barbary macaques live around the site — watch, don't feed.

Afternoon: lunch with a falls view (at your own expense), then the optional small boat ride toward the base of the cascades — seasonal, paid locally, and the closest you will get to the water. Time for the lower viewpoints and photos before the walk back up.

Evening: return drive to Marrakech and drop-off. Short on time on foot? Our guided Ouzoud camel ride covers the same valley at camel pace.

Where it is

The falls in the Atlas foothills northeast of Marrakech. Drag to explore.

What's included

  • Private A/C transport Marrakech ↔ Ouzoud with driver, including fuel
  • Hotel/riad pickup and drop-off in Marrakech
  • Top and bottom viewpoint walks with your driver-guide

What's not included

  • Lunch, drinks and snacks
  • Optional boat ride (seasonal, paid locally)
  • Tips for driver and guide
  • Personal expenses

Frequently asked questions

When is the waterfall at its best?
Spring, when mountain snowmelt swells the flow, and after autumn rains. Summer afternoons are hot but the site stays lively.
How much walking is involved?
The classic loop is a few kilometres with steps down and back up — moderate, with cafés all along the way for rests.
Can I swim at the falls?
Swimming is not part of the programme and conditions vary — the boat ride is the way to get close to the water safely.
